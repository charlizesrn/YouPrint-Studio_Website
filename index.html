ABOUT US YOUPRINT STUDIO

Who We Are

YouPrint Studio is a local printing and graphic design business proudly serving individuals, students, businesses, and organizations in our community. We create a wide range of printed materials – from school reports to personal invitations to business cards and promotional posters – designed to meet your personal, academic, business, and organizational needs.

As a local studio, we’re committed to supporting our neighbors and contributing to our community’s growth.

Printing Tips (choosing paper, finishes, sizes)

Choosing the right paper: From standard bond paper to premium cardstock – learn which type matches your project and budget. We explain the difference between common paper options, so you pick exactly what you need.

Graphic Design

Graphic Design Tips (layout, colors, fonts)

Design Basics: Learn how to arrange text and images for a clear, balanced layout, choose color combinations that complement your brand or project theme, and select the right fonts to create designs that are attractive, readable, and professional.

File Preparation Guide (300 DPI, CMYK, bleed, file types) - Get your files ready for perfect prints

Preparing Your Files for Printing: Learn to set 300 DPI for sharp prints, use CMYK for accurate colors, add bleed to prevent white edges, and choose the right file format (PDF, JPG, PNG, or AI) for the best results.
